Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

ResaleIQ is operated by DTE Solutions LLC (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what we collect when you use the ResaleIQ app and website, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address and password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider). If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic profile information from Google.
  • Inventory you save. Item details you enter or save to your Vault, such as brand, category, condition, size, color, prices, listing titles and status.
  • Content you send for AI processing. Item details you enter to generate listings or price estimates, and photos you take or upload to the Visual Scanner.
  • Usage counts. A count of your AI requests per hour and per day, used to enforce fair-use limits. We do not store the prompts or photos in these records.
  • Technical data. Our hosting and infrastructure providers process standard request data such as IP address, device and browser type, for security and to deliver the service.

We do not use advertising trackers, sell your personal information, or share it for cross-app tracking.

AI processing

ResaleIQ’s listing generator, Flip Pricer and Visual Scanner use AI models provided by Anthropic. When you use these features, the item details you entered, or the photo you selected, are sent to Anthropic to produce a result. We ask for your permission before your first AI request, and you can withdraw it at any time from the Account menu.

Anthropic processes this content as our service provider under its commercial terms, which do not permit using it to train its models. We do not store your photos; they are sent for analysis and discarded. Avoid including personal information about yourself or others in item notes or photos.

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To provide the features you use, including saving your inventory and generating AI results.
  • To prevent abuse, enforce usage limits and keep the service secure.
  • To respond to support requests.

Service providers

We share data only with providers that help us run ResaleIQ:

  • Supabase — authentication, database and server functions.
  • Anthropic — AI processing, as described above.
  • Vercel — website hosting.
  • Google — optional Google sign-in, and web fonts loaded by the app.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account and inventory until you delete them. You can delete individual items at any time, and you can permanently delete your account from Account → Delete account in the app. Deleting your account removes your login, your saved inventory and your usage records. This cannot be undone.

Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your inventory in the app, withdraw AI consent, and delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, export or delete your data. To make a request, contact us at the address below.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to inventory is restricted to the account that owns it, and AI requests require a signed-in account. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password.

Children

ResaleIQ is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above. For significant changes we will notify you in the app.
